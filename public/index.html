    Good to know

    • Weather: about 8–17°C, mostly dry. Bring a light jacket and shoes you can walk in all day.
    • Sunset is around 4:45 PM, so outdoor sights are scheduled before then; Kyoto and Nara days start early.
    • Autumn leaves run late in 2026. Expect early colour in Kyoto and Tokyo; Mt Takao (Nov 17) is the best bet for full colour.
    • Pay with your phone: add a Suica or ICOCA card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet for trains and convenience stores. Carry ¥10,000–20,000 cash for temples and stalls.
    • Taxis make sense for 4: a short ride split four ways often costs about the same as the bus.
    • Before flying: register on Visit Japan Web for faster immigration and customs.
